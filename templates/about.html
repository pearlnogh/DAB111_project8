About the Dataset

Creator: Valakhorasani

Source: Kaggle Dataset

This dataset includes 973 samples of gym data, with details on:

  • Age: The age of the gym member.
  • Gender: The gender of the gym member (Male/Female).
  • Weight (kg): The weight of the gym member in kilograms.
  • Height (m): The height of the gym member in meters.
  • Avg_BPM: The average beats per minute (heart rate) during workout.
  • Session_Duration (hours): The duration of the workout session in hours.
  • Calories_Burned: The total calories burned during the workout session.
  • Workout_Type: The type of workout, including Cardio, Yoga, Strength, and HIIT.
  • Water_Intake (liters): The amount of water consumed during the workout in liters.
  • Workout_Frequency (days/week): The number of days per week the member works out.
  • BMI: The Body Mass Index calculated based on the member's weight and height.
